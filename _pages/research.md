Gauss–Newton methods, Riemannian geometry, adaptive damping, and stochastic optimization and generalization bounds.

- [Non-Asymptotic Generalization and Optimization Bounds for Stochastic Gauss–Newton in Deep Neural Networks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/cayci26a.html)

### Optimization for sequence models

- [Finite-Time Analysis of Gradient Descent for Shallow Transformers](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/arda26a.html)

### Natural policy gradient methods

Entropy regularization, Fisher–Rao geometry, and linear and neural function approximation.

- [Convergence of Entropy-Regularized Natural Policy Gradient with Linear Function Approximation](https://doi.org/10.1137/22M1540156)
- [Optimal Rates of Convergence for Entropy Regularization in Discounted Markov Decision Processes](https://doi.org/10.1093/imaiai/iaaf034)
